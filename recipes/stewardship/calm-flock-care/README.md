# Record calm flock care

Record one quick common-care check and one flock-level normal observation for
eight named birds. A concrete deviation raises attention and prepares only the
observed facts, current care and supplies, and exact source references for human
or professional review. A fresh confirmed egg count may be copied to the
kitchen without exposing the flock. Missing capture stays unknown; the pack
cannot score, rank, value, cull, diagnose, treat, or automate care.
